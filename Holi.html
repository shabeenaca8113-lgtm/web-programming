Holi Festival

Holi is a popular festival of colours celebrated in many parts of India.It represents joy,togetherness and the arrival of spring.Holi has a strong cultural and religious significance.One of the well known stories connected with holi is the story of prahlada and holika.Prahlada was a devoted follower of Lord vishnu,while holika was associated with the attempt to harm him.The story is traditionally understood as representing the victory of good over evil.Holi is celebrated at the end of winter, on the last full moon day of the Hindu luni-solar calendar month, marking the spring, making the date vary with the lunar cycle. The date falls typically in March, but sometimes in late February of the Gregorian calendar.

Celebrations

People celebrate Holi by applying coloured powders and splashing coloured water on each other.Families and friends also share sweets.Holi is mainly celebrated over two days.On the evening before the color celebration,people gather around a bonfire known as holi dahan.The bonfire symbolizes the destruction of evil and the triumph of goodness.
