Trees

Trees are tall and important plants that are found in forests, gardens, parks, and along roadsides. They have strong roots, trunks, branches, and leaves. Trees provide us with oxygen, fruits, wood, shade, and many other useful products.Trees are very important for the environment. They help clean the air, reduce pollution, prevent soil erosion, and provide homes for birds and animals. Trees also help keep the surroundings cool and beautiful. We should protect trees and plant more of them to keep our planet green and healthy.
