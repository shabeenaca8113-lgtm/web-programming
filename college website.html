About Us

MEA college of engineering was established in 2002.It governed by the Muslim Education Association,the college is affiliated with APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University(KTU) and approved by the AICTE.It holds NAAC accreditation and offers diverse undergraduate and postgraduate programs,particularly specializing in high demand fields like Computer Science,Artificial Intelligence, and Automation.

Courses Offered

MEAEC offers a comprehensive range of technical programs,primarly at the Undergraduate(B.Tech,BCA) and Postgraduate(M.Tech,MCA)levels.The courses are structured under the regulations of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University(KTU) and focus heavily on modern engneering domains.
