Onam Festival

Onam is an important festival celebrated in Kerala.It is a harvest festival and is associated with the legendery King Mahabali.It is celebrated for 10 days,with Thiruvonam being the most important day.Onam represents happiness,prosperity,unity,and togetherness.One of the main attractions of Onam is onam sadhya.Onam falls in the month of Chingam, which is the first month according to the Malayalam Calendar. The celebrations mark the Malayalam New Year, are spread over ten days, and conclude with Thiruvonam. The ten days are sequentially known as Atham, Chithira, Chodhi, Vishakam, Anizham, Thriketa, Moolam, Pooradam, Uthradam and Thiruvonam. The first and the last day are particularly important in Kerala and to Malayalee communities elsewhere

Celebrations

People celebrate Onam with floral decorations called Pookalam,traditional food called Onam sadhya,boat races and cultural programs.People decorate their homes with Pookkalam, and prepare Onam sadhya,a special vegetarian feast.Other days have a diverse range of celebrations and activities ranging from boat races, cultural programs, sports competitions, dance events, martial arts, pookkalam (a flower carpet), prayers, shopping, donating time or food for charity to spending time with family over feasts. Men and women wear traditional dress. The Kerala sari or Kasavu sari is particularly wore on this day.
